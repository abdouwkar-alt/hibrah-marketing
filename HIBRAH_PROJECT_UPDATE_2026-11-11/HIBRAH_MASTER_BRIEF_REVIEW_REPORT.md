# HIBRAH MASTER BRIEF REVIEW REPORT

## 1. Executive conclusion

**The available evidence is sufficient to build a useful draft
institutional reference and functional architecture. It is not
sufficient to certify HIBRAH's commercial performance, unit economics,
workshop reliability, customer demand, or complete operating
procedures.**

HIBRAH's brand direction and intended customer are relatively well
articulated. The weaker areas are commercial validation, current
financial inputs, production controls, customer records, and decision
traceability.

The proposed organization should be treated as eight functions and one
Founder authority---not eight immediate hires.

## 6. Organizational recommendation

### 6.1 Retain all eight functions

The eight functions in the assignment are justified as distinct
accountabilities because they own different outcomes and controls. They
should not all be hired as permanent staff at this stage.

### 6.2 Lean operating arrangement

-   Founder temporarily owns final authority and is the accountable
    operator across Marketing, Sales, and Operations.
-   Content/Creative can be Founder-led with AI drafting/research
    support; creative accountability remains separate from calendar
    administration.
-   Administration should begin as a simple process and records system,
    not a dedicated hire.
-   Finance should begin with order-level costing and cash tracking;
    qualified external accounting/tax support is used when required.
-   AI Engineering should be scoped to specific bottlenecks; no custom
    automation program should be funded before workflows and data are
    stable.
-   Independent Review remains a separate function and must not be
    performed as self-approval by the author of the deliverable.
-   Photography, video production, design, legal, and specialist
    technical work should be outsourced project-by-project when needed.

### 6.3 Why this is appropriate

The current business stage does not establish enough order volume,
revenue, or repeatable processes to justify eight dedicated roles.
Hiring ahead of validation would add fixed cost without proof of
workload. However, collapsing functions into one undifferentiated
"assistant" would hide accountability and create unsafe promises. The
correct compromise is distinct function definitions with temporary
shared execution.

## 8. Critical recommendations before growth

1.  Reconcile the current labour cost and rebuild unit economics from
    dated invoices/quotes.
2.  Establish the actual paid customer/order baseline.
3.  Create a lead-to-order register and record lost reasons.
4.  Document the workshop handoff, fitting, QC, rework, and delivery
    process.
5.  Define a realistic service geography and visit cost.
6.  Confirm payment/deposit and customer issue policies.
7.  Do not run or scale paid ads until response handling, capacity,
    cash, and tracking are ready.
8.  Keep public copy neutral on "bespoke" until the Founder decides and
    the actual pattern process is established.
9.  Retrieve the canonical system architecture and record schema before
    claiming institutional integration.
