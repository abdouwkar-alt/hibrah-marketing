## 1. Purpose and source basis

This protocol explains how HIBRAH's eight functions coordinate: who owns each major decision, how tasks move between roles, what information must accompany a handoff, how disagreements are settled, how the Founder receives consolidated reporting, how the Independent Reviewer stays outside the production chain, and how outputs are approved and version-controlled.

The Responsibility Matrix is the primary ownership reference; this protocol adds procedure and does not change ownership.

**Limits.** All six institutional files are unapproved drafts. Their CONFIRMED labels inherit from an AI-compiled brief the Founder has not approved (X-07). No source documents how HIBRAH actually coordinates work between people today beyond the Founder doing most of it, so every procedure below is a RECOMMENDATION.

## 2. Principles

1. The Founder is the final decision-maker and is not one of the eight functions.
2. One owner per activity; the Founder approves anything that becomes a decision, commitment, spend, policy, public statement or publication. Silence is not approval.
3. Roles do not report to one another and do not instruct one another. They exchange information through handoffs, and work is assigned only by the Founder (or a delegate the Founder names in writing).
4. A role never promises a delivery time, capability or price that has not been verified and approved.
5. Role 8 reviews; it does not produce, edit, direct or approve its own work.
6. Conflicts between sources are escalated, not silently resolved.
7. Customer personal data moves only as far as necessary and never to external tools without Founder approval.
8. Lean first: the current stage needs functional accountability, not eight employees (section 14).

## 4. The Founder's current operating involvement

The Founder currently performs multiple activities across the business, including sales/customer communication, operations and workshop coordination, marketing decisions and execution, and content filming/editing. The exact allocation of time and the boundary between hands-on execution and oversight are not established as a formal current-state role map. Therefore, this protocol does not reduce the Founder to only Roles 4 and 5.

Rules:

1. When acting in a hat, the Founder labels it ("as Sales", "as Operations") in the task or note, so records show which function acted.
2. Role 3 records everything the Founder does in those hats so a delegate could take over.
3. The Founder remains the approver. Where a decision is the Founder's as owner and as approver, the Role 8 review (where required) is the only independent check.

## 6. Handoff packet standard

Every handoff between roles (or between a role and the Founder) carries these fields. A handoff missing a required field is returned, not guessed at.

| Field | Content |
|---|---|
| Handoff ID | H-nn from section 7 plus a date and sequence, e.g. H-03-2026-11-12-01 |
| From / To | Sender role and receiver role |
| Trigger | The event that caused the handoff |
| Subject | One sentence |
| Content | The output or data; document name and version |
| Status label | DRAFT, REVIEWED, APPROVED (Founder only), or a data label (CONFIRMED, CURRENT PRACTICE, PROVISIONAL, HYPOTHESIS, RECOMMENDATION, UNKNOWN) |
| Sources | Where each material statement comes from |
| Unknowns and assumptions | Listed, not hidden |
| Approvals required or obtained | What the receiver may and may not do with it |
| Confidentiality | INTERNAL, CONFIDENTIAL, or contains customer personal data (and whether any external tool may receive it) |
| Action requested and by when | What the receiver must do; "none" if informational |

Because the roles run as separate AI conversations, packets are passed by the Founder or by Role 3 on the Founder's instruction. A role cannot assume it can see another role's conversation.

## 8. How tasks move: the commercial chain (illustration, RECOMMENDATION)

1. Inquiry arrives → **R3** logs (H-01) → **R4** replies, qualifies, consults.
2. R4 quotes only from the Founder-approved price list; anything else goes to the Founder (H-02). **No approved list exists today (U-05)**, so every quote currently needs a Founder decision.
3. Customer accepts and pays the deposit per the Founder's policy (**none written: U-06**) → R4 sends the order packet (H-03).
4. **R5** writes the specification, tracks production, inspects, delivers (H-04, H-06); records dates.
5. R4 follows up and collects feedback (H-14). Issues go through H-13.
6. R6 records actual costs and margins; R3 files everything; R1 uses lead and outcome data (H-11).

The boundary between R4 and R5 is order confirmation (adjustment A2). R3 logs and routes, never replies commercially (A3).

## 10. Disagreement resolution

1. **Write it down.** Each side states its position and evidence in the handoff packet fields (section 6). Disagreement about facts is resolved by evidence, not authority.
2. **Evidence or consistency dispute.** The Founder may ask Role 8 for an independent assessment. Role 8 does not take sides in production; it states which claims are verified, unverified or contradicted.
3. **Judgement, policy, price, brand, budget, commitment or public-claim dispute.** Escalate to the Founder with options and consequences.
4. **Decision.** The Founder decides. Role 3 records it in the Decision Register (H-18). If a prior decision is replaced, the old entry is marked OUTDATED, never deleted.
5. **Meanwhile.** The more cautious position applies; no customer commitment, spend or publication is made while a dispute is open.

A role may not override another role's owned decision. If two roles claim the same item, that is a matrix defect: raise it to the Founder.

## 12. How the Independent Reviewer stays outside the production chain

**Structural conditions (RECOMMENDATION, from Architecture A7 and the Role System assignment):**

1. R8 reports only to the Founder.
2. R8 reviews outputs and sources, not the author's reasoning conversation.
3. R8 runs in a separate conversation or instance from any author. The same AI instance that creates a deliverable is never its independent approver.
4. R8 never edits, rewrites, co-authors or directs; owners fix, R8 verifies the fix.
5. R8's findings go to the Founder and the owner at the same time. The owner may reply in writing but cannot alter or withdraw a finding.
6. R8 is not combined with any function, and is not on the approval path for a task except as a review step the Founder requires.
7. Role 3 may deliver packets and hold files but has no influence on conclusions.
8. If any condition fails, R8 declares "independence not met" and stops.

**Honest limit.** If author and reviewer are the same kind of AI system, they may share blind spots. Mitigations: separate sessions, source-first and checklist-based review, Founder sampling of reviews, and an optional external human adviser at key milestones.

**Where review is required (from the matrix, marked R):** segmentation and positioning, go-to-market, marketing budget and ad launch, measurement, content strategy, creation and quality control, public-claim checks, price recommendations and model, customer records and data protection, journey standard, tailor terms, delivery commitment wording, cost analysis, financial risk reporting, tool proposals and implementation, registers and Master Brief maintenance, brand and strategy decisions.

## 13. Approval and version control

### 13.1 Status labels
**DRAFT** (author finished) → **REVIEWED** (Role 8 reviewed, where required) → **APPROVED** (Founder only) → **PUBLISHED / IN FORCE** (Founder confirmed). Replaced versions are **SUPERSEDED / OUTDATED** and kept.

### 13.2 Rules
1. Only the Founder can set APPROVED, PUBLISHED or IN FORCE. Roles may not mark their own output approved.
2. Every document carries: ID, version, status, date, author role, sources used, changes made, open questions, revision history.
3. Version numbers: 0.x while DRAFT; 1.0 on first Founder approval; 1.1, 1.2 for approved corrections; 2.0 for approved structural change.
4. A change that is a decision (a rule, price, policy, claim, structure) needs the Founder's confirmation (quote and date) before the register is updated.
5. Role 3 maintains the document register and the Decision and Unknown Registers and files every approval with date and the words used.
6. Nothing is deleted. Replaced items are marked OUTDATED with the reason and the replacing item.
7. File names: `ROLE_nn_...`, institutional files keep their current names; versions appear in the header, not the file name, until the Founder sets a convention (UNKNOWN).

7. **Role 1**: funnel baseline and competitor review, once lead data starts to exist; no paid test before capacity, cash and tracking.

Test each role with one low-risk task from its charter, review the result with Role 8, then decide whether to keep, revise or pause the role. Activation and testing begin only when the Founder approves.
