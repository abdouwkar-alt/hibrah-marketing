## 2. Material conflicts and reconciliation required

### C-01 --- Tailoring labour cost

-   Earlier project context/master brief: approximately EGP 1,200 plus
    EGP 200 for trousers (scope/date needs confirmation).
-   Pricing calculator (2026-10-24): EGP 1,500 for two-piece and EGP
    1,800 for three-piece.
-   Status: **UNRESOLVED**. May be different definitions, dates, or
    workshop rates.
-   Required resolution: obtain current written/recorded quote per
    garment type and define included work.

### C-02 --- Delivery period

-   Pricing workbook: expected 10--14 days and notes a 14-day customer
    promise.
-   Current Founder rule: do not promise delivery times unless
    operationally verified and explicitly confirmed.
-   Status: older workbook statement is **OUTDATED as customer-facing
    policy**; underlying cycle-time assumption is unverified.
-   Required resolution: use actual order history, workshop capacity,
    and exception data before approving any customer-facing period.

### C-03 --- Brand category terminology

-   Content brief describes HIBRAH as "bespoke".
-   Business Master Brief identifies D-18 as unresolved and notes that
    the actual pattern process is not documented.
-   Current assignment explicitly says use "Tailoring" where relevant;
    public "bespoke" only after Founder decision.
-   Status: use neutral "men's tailoring" internally and externally
    until D-18 is resolved.

### C-04 --- "Workshop" identity vs external production

-   Vision describes developing a tailoring workshop using contemporary
    tools.
-   Current practice relies on external workshops/tailors.
-   Status: not necessarily contradictory if "workshop" is future
    direction. Public descriptions must not imply HIBRAH owns a
    production workshop.

### C-05 --- Content First Assignment

-   Content Writer Brief Sections 0 and 16 instruct first-time models to
    execute the assignment automatically.
-   Current Founder instruction explicitly says it runs only when
    requested.
-   Status: current instruction controls; not executed in this
    assignment.

### C-06 --- Current marketing status

-   Earlier independent audit says pages/publication were not
    established as complete.
-   Later Founder report says Facebook/Instagram exist and pinned intro
    post is published; current project context also reports
    filming/editing reels.
-   Status: later Founder report supersedes earlier "not established"
    wording. Actual platform metrics and commercial outcomes remain
    unknown.

### C-07 --- Financial calculator vs actual performance

-   Calculator has 30% margin and EGP 500 CAC assumptions.
-   No invoices/order-level outcomes verify those assumptions.
-   Status: model assumptions only, not actual margin or CAC.

### C-08 --- Customer counts

-   Earlier briefs say first paying customers not confirmed.
-   Current context still contains no evidence of a completed HIBRAH
    paid order.
-   Status: UNKNOWN, not "zero"; obtain order/payment register.

### C-09 --- Downtown Suit relationship

-   Founder has about six years' experience in Downtown Suit and
    previous customer/marketing experience there.
-   HIBRAH is a separate brand/project.
-   Ownership, legal separation, shared resources, customer-data
    boundaries, and supplier overlap are not established.
-   Status: UNKNOWN; do not mix customer records or brand claims.

### C-10 --- System architecture source availability

-   Prior project conversation describes an institutional system build
    and generated architecture artifacts.
-   Accessible continuity-test artifact states historical canonical
    architecture/template originals were not retrieved and that schema
    conformity remains unresolved.
-   Status: do not claim that the current package is integrated with or
    compliant with the canonical system architecture until original
    documents and schema are available.

### C-11 --- Earlier role decomposition vs current functional architecture

-   Earlier project planning described eight marketing-specialist AI
    roles (Marketing Manager, Market Researcher, Brand Strategist,
    Content Strategist, Content Creator, Social Media Manager,
    Performance Marketer, Marketing Analyst).
-   The current Founder assignment defines a different eight-function
    business architecture: Marketing & Growth, Content & Creative,
    Administration, Sales, Operations, Finance, AI Engineering, and
    Independent Review.
-   Status: the current assignment controls this architecture. The
    earlier specialist decomposition is retained as historical context
    only; no role-specific prompts are created in this phase.
