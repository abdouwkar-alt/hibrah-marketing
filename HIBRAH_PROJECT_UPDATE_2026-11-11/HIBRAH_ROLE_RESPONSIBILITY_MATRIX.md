  Code   Function

  ADM    Business Operations & Administration Manager
  SAL    Sales & Customer Relations Manager

**R = Responsible for doing the work; A = Accountable owner; C =
Consulted; I = Informed.** Founder approval remains mandatory for
matters explicitly listed in the authority column. In a lean setup, the
Founder may temporarily occupy multiple roles, but the function codes
remain distinct.

## 3. Cross-functional decision protocols

### 3.1 Pricing

1.  MKT supplies customer/competitor evidence and willingness-to-pay
    hypotheses.
2.  FIN calculates full cost, gross margin, contribution margin, cash
    timing, and scenarios.
3.  OPS confirms production feasibility, workshop cost,
    visit/fitting/rework exposure, and capacity.
4.  SAL reports customer objections and quote outcomes.
5.  REV checks evidence, assumptions, and internal consistency.
6.  FND approves final price and any public/private communication rule.

No single department owns the entire pricing decision.

### 3.2 Campaign launch and spend

1.  MKT proposes objective, segment, channel, budget, experiment design,
    and stop conditions.
2.  CNT provides the creative package and claim verification.
3.  SAL confirms response handling and lead qualification.
4.  OPS confirms capacity and delivery feasibility.
5.  FIN checks cash exposure and allowable spend.
6.  REV checks assumptions and risk.
7.  FND approves the test and budget.

### 3.3 Customer delivery commitment

1.  OPS provides evidence-based capacity and production estimates.
2.  SAL communicates only the approved customer-facing commitment.
3.  FIN confirms cash/terms implications if relevant.
4.  FND approves policy-level commitments or exceptions.

### 3.4 Technology deployment

1.  Process owner defines the business problem and current manual
    baseline.
2.  AI evaluates tools, data, costs, risks, and maintenance.
3.  ADM/OPS/SAL test the workflow with realistic cases.
4.  REV independently checks failure modes and data handling.
5.  FND approves production deployment and spend.

## 4. Escalation rules

Escalate immediately to Founder when: - customer expectation cannot be
met; - garment fails QC or requires material rework; - a price/discount
exception is requested; - cash needed to fulfill an order is not
available; - workshop capacity is uncertain; - customer data may have
been exposed; - a public claim may be inaccurate; - a campaign may
exceed operational capacity; - any source conflict changes a business
decision.

## 5. No-owner / dual-owner controls

-   Every task must have one accountable function, even if multiple
    functions contribute.
-   Administration is accountable for record completeness, not sales
    conversion.
-   Sales is accountable for customer conversion, not production
    feasibility.
-   Operations is accountable for fulfillment, not market positioning.
-   Finance is accountable for financial analysis, not final business
    strategy.
-   Independent Review is accountable for findings, not implementation
    of corrective action.
-   Founder is accountable for final approval and risk acceptance.
