## 6. Evidence confidence rules

-   **High:** direct current record or explicit Founder decision, with
    date and scope.
-   **Medium:** Founder-reported current practice, or a document whose
    source is clear but underlying event is not independently evidenced.
-   **Low:** older summary, stale model input, search snippet, or
    indirect statement.
-   **Not established:** no usable evidence.

No evidence grade should be mistaken for a truth guarantee. For example,
a Founder-reported workshop arrangement is medium-confidence evidence of
the reported operating model, not proof of workshop quality.
