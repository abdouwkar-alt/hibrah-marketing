## K5. Financial definitions for management

Use consistent definitions: - **Gross margin:** revenue less direct
product costs, with inclusions defined consistently. - **Contribution
margin:** revenue less variable costs attributable to the order,
including allocated acquisition/visit/rework costs where the management
policy includes them. - **CAC:** acquisition spend divided by new paying
customers for the same period and attribution method. - **Cash
conversion:** timing of customer cash received versus fabric, labour,
visit, and other cash paid.

Do not mix gross margin, markup, and contribution margin.

brief uses explicit status and approval fields but does not claim
conformity to the unresolved canonical system schema.

and evidence; 2. identify affected sections/files; 3. supersede prior

register and revision history; 5. be reviewed for cross-document
consistency.
