## 7. Metadata-based relevance flags

The following are candidates for closer visual review, not automatic rejections:

- **095 — Jungle jacket with black jeans:** likely casual outerwear rather than a tailored suit reference.
- **096 — Brown Harrington with black jeans:** likely casualwear, weak fit for a classic suit library.
- **097 — Cotton suit with shirt:** potentially relevant; verify that the image is a complete tailored suit and not an unrelated casual set.
- **098 — Cotton jacket with trousers:** potentially useful as tailoring/styling reference; verify whether it is a suit or separates.
- **099 — Cotton jacket with denim:** likely casual styling, weaker relevance to the suit-focused collection.
- **100 — Shorts with navy crewneck:** likely outside the intended suit/tailoring scope.
- **041–043 and other editorial diagrams/banners:** verify that the file is a useful reference image rather than a title graphic, collage, or article header.
- **Reda 1865 group:** multiple files share generic or repeated page-level descriptions. Inspect visual variety and distinguish fabric swatches from campaign/editorial graphics.
- **Drake's groups:** inspect for near-duplicate angles and verify that each image actually shows a suit or relevant tailoring detail.

These flags are derived from filenames, alt text, page titles, and metadata only. They are not visual findings.

## 8. Source and rights control

All 100 records remain `rights_status: NOT_CLEARED` and `intended_use: INTERNAL_CREATIVE_REVIEW_ONLY`.

A source URL is provenance, not a usage licence. Before any external publication, campaign use, redistribution, or Canva upload, complete item-level rights review and record the applicable permission, licence, or approved basis. Creative relevance review and rights review must remain separate.

## 9. Recommended Founder review sequence

1. Open the 100-image index and make the visual keep/delete selection.
2. Prioritize the obvious scope candidates (095, 096, 099, 100) and the low-resolution group.
3. Check visually similar images within the Reda, Drake's, and Permanent Style source groups.
4. Keep the selected files in `drafts` until the selection is complete.
5. After Founder selection, run a fresh inventory against the remaining files, reconcile the manifest and categories, then conduct item-level rights review. Only then prepare a proposed move to an approved library.
