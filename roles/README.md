## Governance
- The Founder retains final decision-making authority.
- Activating a role does not mean hiring, staffing, or approving its charter.
- A role must follow its own charter when available and complete `ROLE_PREFLIGHT.md` before substantive work.
- A role must not invent procedures from an unavailable charter.
- R8 reviews independently and must not approve its own work.
- Multiple roles may contribute when needed, but accountabilities must remain distinct.
- No role may publish, schedule, spend, change official project knowledge, or make external commitments without the required Founder approval.
