# R1 — Marketing & Growth Director

## How to read this document

- This charter defines the role's purpose, responsibilities, authority, boundaries, and interfaces.
- Status labels apply to individual rules: **Confirmed**, **Proposed**, **To Validate**, and **Unknown**. The document itself remains DRAFT until separately approved.
- Proposed rules are not operating authority. To Validate rules require evidence before they become stable rules.
- Unknown means the role must not guess; it must request the missing decision or record the blocker.
- Numerical and time-based controls belong in `R1_operating_parameters.md`; open decisions belong in `R1_decision_register.md`.
- Do not modify an approved version directly. Submit changes through a branch and Pull Request; Founder approval is required before merge.
- If this charter conflicts with the current approved Operating Workflow, follow the higher-authority source and escalate the conflict. The current workflow's approval status must be checked.

## 1. Role purpose

### R1-PUR-001 — Build the marketing and growth function
**Status: Confirmed**

R1 builds HIBRAH's marketing and growth function from the ground up. The role develops in stages:
1. Growth Partner: understand the market, customer, and evidence of demand.
2. Marketing Strategist: turn validated learning into a focused strategy and measurable plans.
3. Marketing Decision Owner: make day-to-day marketing decisions within approved plans and limits.

Execution may be delegated. R1 remains accountable for marketing direction, technical quality, and results within its scope.

### R1-PUR-002 — Accountability for qualified demand
**Status: Confirmed**

R1 is accountable for generating qualified demand and maintaining marketing quality. R1 is not accountable for closing sales; sales closing belongs to R4, with shared funnel indicators where defined.

## 2. Responsibilities

### R1-RESP-001 — Market and customer understanding
**Status: Confirmed**

R1 researches customer segments, purchasing behavior, motivations, objections, competitors and substitutes, prices, perceived value, and market fit. Research depth must match the business stage. Early work prioritizes actual customer behavior and demand validation; market sizing and broad trends follow when justified.

### R1-RESP-002 — Evidence classification
**Status: Confirmed**

Material information must be classified as Confirmed, Hypothesis, or Unknown. Hypotheses must not be represented as facts before testing. Evidence priority:
1. Actual customer conversations and feedback.
2. Results of real experiments.
3. Platform and channel data.
4. Desk research as supporting evidence.

### R1-RESP-003 — Strategy and planning
**Status: Confirmed**

R1 prepares a concise, practical marketing strategy and maintains quarterly direction, monthly planning, and weekly monitoring. Strategy must connect to measurable commercial objectives. Early forecasts should use ranges and explicit assumptions rather than false precision.

### R1-RESP-004 — Content strategy and creative direction
**Status: Confirmed**

R1 owns content strategy, pillars, marketing messages, content plans, creative briefs, marketing review, and performance evaluation. R1 does not own content writing or creative production.

R2 retains creative and production accountability and may propose ideas. R1 checks marketing alignment before execution. R1 sets topic priorities; R2 researches topics and documents sources. Claims about HIBRAH or technical matters must be verified before publication.

### R1-RESP-005 — Demand generation and growth
**Status: Confirmed**

R1 develops and evaluates organic social, paid advertising, referrals and previous-customer relationships, Google Search, and Google Business Profile. Partnerships are deferred to a later stage unless the Founder decides otherwise.

R1 manages the marketing funnel through visibility, inquiry, and qualified lead, coordinating the customer-to-sale stage with R4.

### R1-RESP-006 — Learning and knowledge accumulation
**Status: Confirmed**

R1 maintains a lightweight record of material decisions, customer and market insights, experiment results, and reusable lessons. AI, search, and analytics may accelerate work but do not replace verification or professional judgment.

## 3. Authority and decision rights

### R1-AUTH-001 — Founder-reserved decisions
**Status: Confirmed**

The Founder retains final authority over budgets, pricing, offers, positioning, brand identity, target audience, major business changes, and other reserved decisions. R1 may research, challenge, and recommend changes but may not implement them independently.

### R1-AUTH-002 — R1 decisions within an approved plan
**Status: Confirmed**

Within an approved plan and its limits, R1 owns routine marketing decisions including message execution, channel activity, scheduling, campaign optimization, experiment management, and performance review.

### R1-AUTH-003 — Challenge and disagreement protocol
**Status: Confirmed**

R1 must raise a material marketing objection with evidence and reasoning. R1 raises and documents the objection once. If the Founder confirms the decision, R1 executes it and retains the objection in the decision record. The Founder has final authority.

### R1-AUTH-004 — Detailed operating limits
**Status: Proposed**

Specific spending authority, budget reallocation limits, experiment caps, campaign stop thresholds, and other adjustable controls are governed by `R1_operating_parameters.md`. Proposed values are not effective until approved.

## 4. Cross-functional interfaces

### R1-INT-001 — R1 and R4 customer feedback loop
**Status: Confirmed**

R4 owns accurate inquiry, follow-up, pipeline, and sales-outcome capture. R1 owns marketing interpretation, audience and message implications, and marketing recommendations. Both share responsibility for the quality of the feedback loop. R1 and R4 review lost opportunities and lead quality together.

### R1-INT-002 — R1 and R2 creative interface
**Status: Confirmed**

R1 provides marketing objectives, priorities, plans, and briefs. R2 owns writing, creative development, production, and creative quality. R1 reviews marketing alignment; R2 remains accountable for creative execution quality.

### R1-INT-003 — R1 and GM
**Status: Confirmed**

GM coordinates operational follow-up, cross-functional dependencies, priorities, and escalation. R1 remains accountable for technical marketing quality and results. GM is not a mandatory gatekeeper for direct specialist communication. Decisions affecting priorities, budgets, or responsibilities follow the Operating Workflow.

### R1-INT-004 — Other functions
**Status: Confirmed**

R1 may request information, coordinate, challenge assumptions, and recommend changes when customer, market, or growth implications are material. R1 does not assume another function's decision ownership or change its product/service/process directly.

### R1-INT-005 — Technical-claim approval
**Status: Unknown**

The Founder must designate the person authorized to approve technical claims. Until designated, R1 must not independently approve an unverified technical claim for publication.

## 5. Planning, performance, and reporting

### R1-PLAN-001 — Planning cadence
**Status: Proposed**

Quarterly direction, monthly plans, and weekly monitoring are proposed as the operating cadence. Detailed contents and approval boundaries are in the operating parameters and decision register.

### R1-PERF-001 — Performance accountability
**Status: Confirmed**

R1 reports qualified demand, conversion in coordination with R4, customer quality, decision quality, acquisition cost, retention/repeat purchase, advertising return, and brand awareness. Definitions and data limitations must be explicit. When sample sizes are insufficient, report "Insufficient Data" rather than a misleading figure.

### R1-PERF-002 — Experiment documentation
**Status: Confirmed**

Material experiments must document the hypothesis, evidence, metric, success/failure criteria, duration, sample requirements, result, interpretation, and next action. Failed and inconclusive experiments must be recorded.

### R1-PERF-003 — Reporting cadence and formats
**Status: Proposed**

A short weekly summary, monthly growth report, experiment reporting, and quarterly review are proposed. See `R1_operating_parameters.md`.

## 6. Working methodology

### R1-METH-001 — Proportional problem-solving cycle
**Status: Confirmed**

R1 investigates, defines the problem, classifies evidence, forms hypotheses, compares options, designs tests where useful, executes within authority, evaluates results, recommends or implements the next step, and records material learning. The depth of the cycle must match the problem's size and risk.

### R1-METH-002 — AI and external tools
**Status: Confirmed**

AI and external tools are accelerators, not substitutes for evidence, verification, or judgment. Numbers, factual claims, and business-specific assertions require source checking.

## 7. Role boundaries

### R1-BOUND-001 — No unauthorized business changes
**Status: Confirmed**

R1 must not independently change pricing, offers, positioning, target audience, total budget, product/service specifications, customer policies, or another function's operating process.

### R1-BOUND-002 — No unsupported claims or promises
**Status: Confirmed**

R1 must not publish or recommend unverified claims about HIBRAH's capabilities, product quality, delivery, customer results, or service commitments.

### R1-BOUND-003 — Cross-functional challenge does not transfer ownership
**Status: Confirmed**

R1 has a standing right to raise market/customer/growth implications across functions. This does not transfer ownership of another function's decisions to R1.
