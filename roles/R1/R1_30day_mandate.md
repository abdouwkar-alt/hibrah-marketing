# R1 — Initial 30-Day Mandate

## Status and use

This is a time-bound starting mandate, not a permanent role definition. It remains DRAFT until Founder approval. Numerical targets and budget are intentionally not invented. After the cycle, archive this file and create a new mandate only if needed.

## Priority 1 — Validate demand and understand the customer
**Status: Confirmed**

- Review accessible market, customer, and historical business information relevant to HIBRAH.
- Identify the most important customer and demand hypotheses.
- Collect and classify actual inquiries, questions, objections, and purchase outcomes.
- Identify repeated purchase motivations, objections, and reasons for lost opportunities.
- Present a demand-validation definition and numerical threshold for Founder decision.

**Completion evidence:** A concise evidence summary distinguishing confirmed findings, hypotheses, and unknowns; an approved demand-validation threshold or a clearly documented blocker.

## Priority 2 — Generate actual qualified inquiries
**Status: Confirmed**

- Execute approved acquisition actions.
- Measure inquiry quality, not only reach, engagement, or views.
- Coordinate with R4 to connect inquiry sources, qualification, follow-up, and sales outcomes.

**Completion evidence:** A traceable record of inquiries and their current classifications/outcomes, with no unsupported claims of demand validation.

## Priority 3 — Establish a simple acquisition system
**Status: Confirmed**

- Assess the initial channel options.
- Recommend a manageable set of two or three channels.
- Define a small, measurable test for each selected channel.
- Avoid expanding channels before evaluating current evidence.

**Completion evidence:** A channel plan with hypotheses, owners, measurement approach, required approvals, and budget limits.

## Priority 4 — Prepare a short evidence-based marketing strategy
**Status: Confirmed**

- Prepare a concise strategy based on actual learning.
- Connect recommendations to measurable objectives and explicit assumptions.
- Present alternatives and one recommended direction for the next cycle.

**Completion evidence:** A short strategy document with evidence, assumptions, priorities, and decisions required.

## Parallel work — Brand and content
**Status: Confirmed**

Brand and content development continue in parallel where they support customer understanding and demand generation. They must not displace the commercial validation priorities.

## Dependencies and open items

- Budget and financial authority: see R1-DEC-301 and R1-DEC-302.
- Demand-validation threshold and first-month targets: see R1-DEC-303 and R1-DEC-304.
- Technical-claim approval: see R1-DEC-305.
- Cross-functional interfaces with R5/R6/R7: see R1-DEC-306.

## Review

At the end of the 30-day cycle, review actual evidence, customer outcomes, channel performance, decisions, and lessons. Mark this mandate completed/superseded and preserve it as history; do not silently rewrite the original mandate.
