# R1 — Decision & Unknown Register

## Reading rules

This is the single R1-specific register for open decisions and validation items. It does not replace the company-wide `DECISION_LOG.md`. A status change requires explicit approval or evidence appropriate to the item; never change a status merely because a proposal appears in a draft. Preserve IDs permanently.

Status vocabulary:
- **Confirmed:** explicitly decided by the Founder.
- **Proposed:** recommendation awaiting Founder approval; not effective.
- **To Validate:** requires evidence or a test before becoming a stable rule.
- **Unknown:** required information or decision is not available.

## Confirmed decisions

- **R1-DEC-001 — Role development sequence**
  - Status: Confirmed
  - Decision: R1 starts as a growth partner validating demand, then develops strategy, then takes routine marketing decisions within approved plans.
  - Source: Founder answers to R1 role-establishment interview.
- **R1-DEC-002 — Founder final authority and challenge protocol**
  - Status: Confirmed
  - Decision: Founder retains final authority. R1 may object once with evidence, document the objection, and execute if the Founder confirms the decision.
- **R1-DEC-003 — Marketing versus sales accountability**
  - Status: Confirmed
  - Decision: R1 owns qualified demand and marketing quality; R4 owns sales closing. Shared funnel indicators and feedback loops apply.
- **R1-DEC-004 — Reserved and delegated decisions**
  - Status: Confirmed
  - Decision: Founder owns budget, pricing/offers, positioning, and target audience approval. R1 owns messages, channels, and campaigns within an approved plan. Anything outside the plan returns to the Founder.
- **R1-DEC-005 — Evidence discipline**
  - Status: Confirmed
  - Decision: Prioritize actual customer statements, experiment results, platform data, then desk research. Classify findings as Confirmed, Hypothesis, or Unknown.
- **R1-DEC-006 — Content and creative interface**
  - Status: Confirmed
  - Decision: R1 owns content strategy, pillars, messages, plan, brief, marketing review, and performance assessment, not writing. R2 owns creative execution and quality.
- **R1-DEC-007 — Planning and reporting intent**
  - Status: Confirmed
  - Decision: Use concise strategy and quarterly/monthly/weekly planning logic; connect strategy to numerical objectives. The exact operating cadence remains Proposed pending operational confirmation.
- **R1-DEC-008 — Growth channels and experimentation**
  - Status: Confirmed
  - Decision: Initial channel scope includes organic social, paid advertising, referrals/previous-customer relationships, and Google Search/Business Profile. Partnerships are deferred. New channels should be tested in small controlled experiments.
- **R1-DEC-009 — Cross-functional interfaces**
  - Status: Confirmed
  - Decision: R1 and R4 share customer feedback-loop quality; GM owns operational coordination; R1 owns technical marketing quality and results; R1 may contribute to other functions when customer/market/growth impact is material.
- **R1-DEC-010 — Learning and decision quality**
  - Status: Confirmed
  - Decision: Document material experiments and lessons, use AI as an accelerator subject to review, and assess decision quality by evidence and process as well as outcomes.
- **R1-DEC-011 — First 30-day priorities**
  - Status: Confirmed
  - Decision: Prioritize (1) demand validation and customer understanding, (2) actual qualified inquiries, (3) a simple acquisition system using two or three channels, and (4) a short strategy based on learning. Brand and content work continue in parallel.
- **R1-DEC-012 — Documentation architecture**
  - Status: Confirmed
  - Decision: Adopt a modular R1 documentation package: stable role charter, adjustable operating parameters, centralized decision/unknown register, time-bound 30-day mandate, README, and CHANGELOG. Use stable rule IDs, front matter metadata, and branch/PR review. The approved architecture does not approve the substantive R1 charter or its Proposed rules.
  - Source: Founder approval in the conversation on 2026-11-12.

## Proposed decisions

- **R1-DEC-101 — Qualified Lead definition**
  - Status: Proposed
  - Proposal: Specific inquiry, within service area, near-term purchase intent, no obvious budget/price mismatch, and valid contact method. Initial classes: Qualified / Potential / Not Qualified; R4 confirms after first contact.
  - Related parameter: R1-PAR-001.
- **R1-DEC-102 — Demand validation formula**
  - Status: Proposed
  - Proposal: N qualified inquiries within a defined period, from at least two channels, plus at least one actual purchase decision.
  - Related parameter: R1-PAR-002.
- **R1-DEC-103 — Budget allocation controls**
  - Status: Proposed
  - Proposal: Founder approval above approximately 20–25% channel reallocation; new-channel experiments capped around 10–15% of approved budget; campaign stop rule at twice target cost per inquiry after sufficient data.
  - Related parameters: R1-PAR-004 to R1-PAR-007.
- **R1-DEC-104 — Decision boundaries within approved plan**
  - Status: Proposed
  - Proposal: R1 handles messages, scheduling, campaign optimization, weak-campaign pauses, and tests within limits. Founder approval for positioning/tone changes, offers/prices, new budgeted channels, new claims, partnerships, and irreversible public commitments.
- **R1-DEC-105 — Experiment protocol**
  - Status: Proposed
  - Proposal: Predefine hypothesis, primary metric, success/failure thresholds, duration, and minimum sample. Outcomes are Successful / Failed / Inconclusive; extend an inconclusive test once only.
- **R1-DEC-106 — Initial channel priority**
  - Status: Proposed / To Validate
  - Proposal: Begin with high-intent/low-cost opportunities through Google, referrals/previous customers, and organic social; paid advertising follows evidence of what works. Validate against actual HIBRAH baseline and channel feasibility.
- **R1-DEC-107 — Single source of truth for inquiry data**
  - Status: Proposed
  - Proposal: R1 designs fields and definitions; R4 enters inquiry/sales/loss data; GM monitors compliance; one shared record is used.
- **R1-DEC-108 — Decision quality rubric**
  - Status: Proposed
  - Proposal: Five criteria scored 0–2, maximum 10, with monthly sample review.
- **R1-DEC-109 — Planning cadence and approvals**
  - Status: Proposed
  - Proposal: Quarterly direction; monthly objectives, channel/content plan, experiments, and budget; weekly short summary. Founder approves budget/offer/positioning components; R1 owns other in-plan details with GM visibility.
- **R1-DEC-110 — KPI definitions**
  - Status: Proposed
  - Proposal: Definitions in R1 operating parameters, with insufficient-data caveats and cohort/date rules.
- **R1-DEC-111 — R1/R4 data handoff**
  - Status: Proposed
  - Proposal: R4 enters inquiry data on the same working day, within 24 hours; R1 designs lost-reason taxonomy; weekly sample review.
- **R1-DEC-112 — R1/R2 content workflow**
  - Status: Proposed
  - Proposal: R1 approves in-plan content plan; Founder reviews new positioning/offers/claims; R2 documents sources; technical authority approves technical claims; R1 reviews before publication and after approximately seven days.
- **R1-DEC-113 — Reporting and escalation**
  - Status: Proposed
  - Proposal: Weekly summary, monthly growth report, quarterly review; urgent escalation within 24 hours for reputation risks, published incorrect claims, or budget overruns.
