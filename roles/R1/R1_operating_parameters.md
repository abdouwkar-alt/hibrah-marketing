# R1 — Operating Parameters

## How to use this file

This file contains adjustable numeric, time-based, and measurement parameters. It does not grant authority by itself. Every item must carry a status. Proposed values are inactive until Founder approval. Unknown values must not be invented. Update this file through a branch and Pull Request; record material changes in CHANGELOG.md and the decision register.

## 1. Lead qualification and demand validation

- **R1-PAR-001 — Initial qualified lead criteria**
  - Status: Proposed / To Validate
  - Initial criteria: specific inquiry (price, appointment, measurement, or service details); within HIBRAH's service area; known or near-term purchase intent; no obvious budget/price mismatch; valid contact method.
  - Initial labels: Qualified / Potential / Not Qualified.
  - R4 confirms or adjusts classification after first direct contact.
  - Review after the first 20–30 inquiries.
- **R1-PAR-002 — Demand-validation threshold**
  - Status: Unknown
  - Proposed structure: N qualified inquiries within a defined period, from at least two channels, plus at least one actual purchase decision.
  - N and observation period: Founder decision required.
- **R1-PAR-003 — First-month numerical targets**
  - Status: Unknown
  - Founder sets targets after reviewing the available baseline and budget.

## 2. Budget controls

- **R1-PAR-004 — Budget reallocation**
  - Status: Proposed / To Validate
  - Proposed trigger for Founder approval: reallocating more than approximately 20–25% from one channel to another.
- **R1-PAR-005 — New-channel experiment allocation**
  - Status: Proposed / To Validate
  - Proposed cap: approximately 10–15% of the approved budget.
- **R1-PAR-006 — Total budget and campaign cap**
  - Status: Unknown
  - Founder sets the total budget, R1 financial authority, and any single-campaign approval threshold.
- **R1-PAR-007 — Campaign stop threshold**
  - Status: Proposed / To Validate
  - Proposed rule: pause a campaign if cost per inquiry exceeds twice the agreed target after sufficient data has accumulated. Target and minimum evidence threshold must be set before launch.

## 3. Experiment parameters

- **R1-PAR-008 — Experiment record**
  - Status: Proposed
  - Each material test records hypothesis, primary metric, success threshold, failure threshold, duration, minimum sample, result, interpretation, and next action.
- **R1-PAR-009 — Inconclusive experiment extension**
  - Status: Proposed
  - An inconclusive test may be extended once, with a documented reason.
- **R1-PAR-010 — New-channel entry conditions**
  - Status: Proposed
  - Assess current channels first; document a hypothesis, small budget, duration, and accountable owner before testing one new channel in a cycle.

## 4. Data and service levels

- **R1-PAR-011 — Inquiry data entry**
  - Status: Proposed
  - R4 enters inquiry data on the same working day, no later than 24 hours after receipt.
- **R1-PAR-012 — Lead review**
  - Status: Proposed
  - R1 and R4 conduct a short weekly review of a sample of leads and conversion outcomes.
- **R1-PAR-013 — Content performance review**
  - Status: Proposed
  - Review content performance approximately seven days after publication.
- **R1-PAR-014 — Repeat-purchase measurement period**
  - Status: To Validate
  - Define the measurement period after observing actual customer purchase cycles.

## 5. KPI definitions

- **R1-PAR-015 — Qualified inquiries**
  - Status: Proposed
  - Count inquiries meeting the agreed qualification criteria in the period.
- **R1-PAR-016 — Conversion rate**
  - Status: Proposed
  - New customers divided by qualified inquiries, grouped by inquiry entry date.
- **R1-PAR-017 — Customer quality**
  - Status: Proposed
  - Qualified inquiries as a share of total inquiries, plus the share progressing to an offer or meeting.
- **R1-PAR-018 — Customer acquisition cost**
  - Status: Proposed
  - Marketing spend divided by new customers. Show "Insufficient Data" when volume is too small for a meaningful reading.
- **R1-PAR-019 — Retention and repeat purchase**
  - Status: To Validate
  - Customers who purchased again during the defined period divided by the relevant customer base. Period and cohort rules remain open.
- **R1-PAR-020 — ROAS**
  - Status: Proposed
  - Revenue attributed to advertising divided by advertising spend, only where attribution is sufficiently reliable.

## 6. Decision quality score

- **R1-PAR-021 — Decision quality rubric**
  - Status: Proposed
  - Review a monthly sample of material decisions.
  - Score each criterion 0–2:
    1. Clear hypothesis.
    2. Evidence correctly classified.
    3. Alternatives compared.
    4. Success criteria defined before execution.
    5. Documentation and follow-up completed.
  - Maximum score: 10. Evaluate decision discipline and learning, not outcome alone.

## 7. Reporting cadence

- **R1-PAR-022 — Weekly summary**
  - Status: Proposed
  - New and qualified inquiries, spend, active experiments, obstacles, and decisions required.
- **R1-PAR-023 — Monthly growth report**
  - Status: Proposed
  - Performance against objectives, channel/campaign results, lead quality, conversion, customer insights, and recommendations.
- **R1-PAR-024 — Quarterly review**
  - Status: Proposed
  - Direction, hypotheses, channel performance, budget, and expansion/stop decisions.

## 8. Escalation timing

- **R1-PAR-025 — Urgent escalation**
  - Status: Proposed
  - Escalate within 24 hours for material reputation risks, published incorrect claims, or budget overruns. Follow the current approved company escalation path; if unclear, contact the Founder and GM.

## Change control

Changes to parameter values do not automatically amend the stable role charter. Every change must preserve the parameter ID, record its previous value where material, identify the approving authority, and update the decision register and CHANGELOG.md.
