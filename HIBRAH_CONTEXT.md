# HIBRAH — Brand & Business Context
Version: 0.1
Last updated: 2026-10-31
Status: Working reference. Confirmed facts and provisional assumptions are separated.

## 1. Brand identity
- Arabic name: حِبرة
- English name: HIBRAH
- Business: bespoke / made-to-measure men's clothing in Egypt, with suits as the primary product.
- Brand identity: فخامة وحِرفية.
- Brand slogan:
  - حُسْنٌ يُرْتَدَى
  - فَصَّلْنَاهُ لِيَسُرَّكَ
- Visual direction: brown and cream only. Do not introduce olive/green or gold. Arabic wordmark is primary, with HIBRAH beneath it.
- Brand personality: quiet, private, professional, considered, craft-led. Avoid loud luxury cues and aggressive sales language.

## 2. Meaning and principles
### حُسْنٌ يُرْتَدَى
HIBRAH aims to make the best product it can for the person who will wear it. This is not equivalent to the most expensive fabric or the highest price. The best choice depends on use, fit, comfort, durability, material, execution, and the customer's preferences.

### فَصَّلْنَاهُ لِيَسُرَّكَ
The customer's experience matters before, during, and after the order. The brand should understand the customer's needs, help them make informed choices, respect their preferences, save time where possible, and care about the finished result.

### Non-negotiable principles
1. Honesty comes before quality claims and before profit maximization.
2. Never exaggerate the product, fabric, workmanship, service, or guarantee.
3. Do not push a customer toward a more expensive option merely to increase revenue.
4. HIBRAH may consciously accept a lower margin to deliver a better and fair result, but must not price below its real sustainable cost through careless calculation.
5. The customer's best interest and the integrity of the finished product matter more than closing every sale.
6. Marketing should accurately express the actual experience; it must not promise what operations cannot deliver.

## 3. Products and services
- Primary product: men's bespoke / made-to-measure suits.
- Potential adjacent products: jackets, trousers, shirts.
- Fabric options discussed: Turkish, wool, Italian.
- Service elements discussed: helping the customer choose fabric suitable for their use; discussing preferences and details; measurements; tailoring and delivery; arranging home visits where feasible; recording measurements/preferences for future orders.
- Do not state any service as universally available until its operational conditions are confirmed.
- Do not invent fabric composition, origin, mill, grade, construction method, delivery time, alteration policy, warranty, or quality specifications.

## 5. Customer segments
Primary audience: men who wear formal clothing frequently, value fit and workmanship, and appreciate a professional personal service and a long-term relationship.

Potential occupational groups include:
- lawyers and legal professionals
- doctors and consultants
- banking and finance professionals
- executives, managers, and business owners
- media professionals and presenters
- university professors
These are hypotheses for targeting, not a claim that occupation alone makes someone a suitable customer.

Other segments:
- people whose body shape or measurements make ready-to-wear difficult
- occasion-led buyers
These may be addressed with distinct content or campaigns, but should not displace the core positioning.

Geography: not Cairo-only in principle. Travel and appointment feasibility must be evaluated by distance, travel cost, clustering appointments, and operational capacity. Do not promise nationwide home visits without confirmation.

## 6. Customer value
Potential value propositions, to be used only when the actual process supports them:
- considered fabric selection based on intended use
- attention to fit, construction, finishing, and details
- guidance based on tailoring knowledge without imposing taste
- a personal and professional ordering experience
- convenience through appointment coordination and feasible home visits
- retaining customer measurements/preferences for future orders

The competitive advantage should be proven in the finished product and the customer's experience, not merely asserted in advertising.

## 8. Writing and language preferences
- Write in natural, clear Egyptian Arabic unless another language is requested.
- Tone: composed, direct, professional, understated, and human.
- No forced friendliness, over-politeness, grand claims, or generic luxury clichés.
- Prefer concrete wording over abstract brand language.
- Keep customer-facing copy concise unless the format requires detail.
- Do not use the word "فلسفة" in customer-facing copy.
- Avoid these disliked phrases/words in customer-facing copy: "البدلة الحلوة"، "قصات"، "على قد توقعاتك"، "غايتنا"، "حسن ظنك"، "سعيد"، "راضي". Also avoid awkward repeated wording such as "تفاصيل التفصيل".
- Avoid filler greetings and stock phrases such as "يسعدنا خدمتكم" unless explicitly requested.
- Do not force a CTA into every post.
- Do not publish a price, discount, guarantee, turnaround time, or service condition unless approved.

## 12. Confirmed operating model — founder clarification, 2026-10-31
This section supersedes earlier open questions wherever the founder has now provided a clear answer.

### Inquiry channels
- Customers may initiate contact through Facebook, Instagram, WhatsApp, or phone call.
- A website with an inquiry/booking form is a future possibility, not a current channel. Do not imply that it exists.

### Appointment and location
- The workshop can be used as the meeting point when agreed with the customer.
- The workshop is a worksite, not a dedicated customer reception space. Do not describe it as a showroom or polished customer-facing branch.
- Home visits are preferred where practical, but must be coordinated according to location, availability, and schedule.
- For customers outside Cairo, first check whether travel is feasible and agree on an available appointment. Do not promise a visit automatically.

### Order timeline and fittings
- Customer-facing expected completion time: within 14 days from order confirmation.
- Order confirmation is when the customer selects the fabric and pays the 25% deposit. This may happen at the workshop or during a home visit; the 14-day period starts from that day.
- Example: if fabric selection and deposit happen on Sunday, count the 14 days from that Sunday.
- Internal operational aim: complete in 10 days or less when possible. Do not promise the internal target as a guarantee.
- A fitting between initial measurements and delivery is a standard part of the process.
- The normal sequence is initial measurement, an intermediate fitting, then final delivery.
- If adjustments are needed, an additional fitting may take place; delivery may occur at the third visit at the latest under the described normal operating plan.
- Describe the fitting schedule accurately and do not promise an exact number of visits for every order if the customer's case requires adjustment.

### Price and inclusions — founder-confirmed current starting prices
- Turkish: starts at EGP 6,000.
- English wool: starts at EGP 9,000.
- Italian: starts at EGP 11,000.
- The stated suit price includes the complete suit as offered by HIBRAH; do not invent a breakdown or excluded components.
- These are starting prices, not a promise that every fabric or specification in a category has the same final price.
- Do not haggle or invent discretionary discounts. Any exceptional pricing should be explicitly approved by the founder.
- A trousers-only / separate tailoring request is assessed individually for now. If demand becomes sufficiently consistent, HIBRAH may create a defined price list for that category.

### Payment schedule
- 25% of the agreed suit price at order confirmation / start of work.
- 25% at the fitting stage.
- Remaining 50% at delivery.
- The founder may agree a different arrangement with a customer in an individual case. Do not present exceptions as the standard policy.

### Alterations and after-sales contact
- Delivery should take place after the customer's adjustments have been addressed and the final result is checked.
- Customers may contact HIBRAH after delivery if they need help or wish to discuss a further adjustment.
- A formal written alteration policy, including how to distinguish a correction related to the original work from a new change requested after delivery, has not yet been defined. Do not promise unlimited free alterations or a warranty unless the founder confirms the terms.
