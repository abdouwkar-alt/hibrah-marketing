# HIBRAH — Organizational Structure

## 1. Purpose

This document defines HIBRAH's current functional structure, accountability, authority and reporting relationships. It is derived from the approved Operating Workflow. It does not create a department merely because a workflow contains a task.

This is a functional structure for the current stage. It does not imply that every function is staffed by a separate employee or that HIBRAH has already hired a complete team. One person may temporarily perform more than one function, provided the responsibilities remain clear.

## 2. Structure

**Founder**
- **General Manager (GM)**
  - Marketing
  - Creative
  - Sales & Customer Relations
  - Finance & Accounting
  - Development & R&D

The functions collaborate horizontally as required by the Operating Workflow. The GM coordinates company-wide priorities and execution. The Founder may work directly with any function and retains final authority.

## 3. Founder

### Purpose
Own the business direction and final decisions while establishing and improving the operating system.

### Responsibilities
- Set and approve business goals and strategic direction.
- Approve the Brand Foundation, Operating Workflow, Organizational Structure and material changes.
- Approve marketing strategy and other plans where Founder approval is required.
- Retain final authority over brand, pricing, budgets, customer policy and exceptional commitments.
- Review outputs and results directly.
- Currently perform direct technical review of work and delivered results as a temporary control until the relevant standards and results are established.
- Set review/reporting sessions at the current stage.
- Decide when authority or review arrangements should be delegated or changed.

The Founder is not expected to produce every specialist deliverable or personally execute every task. Functions are expected to act within approved goals, plans and responsibilities.

## 4. General Manager (GM)

### Purpose
Convert approved goals and plans into clear operating priorities and coordinated execution across functions.

### Responsibilities
- Set priorities for each operating period based on approved goals and plans.
- Determine which functions and workstreams need attention now and what should wait.
- Translate priorities into tasks, owners, sequence and target timing.
- Coordinate cross-functional dependencies and handoffs.
- Follow up on progress, blockers, risks and completion.
- Request and collect each function's own status/performance report.
- Consolidate reports and present the operating picture to the Founder.
- Track actions and decisions arising from Founder review sessions.
- Escalate material conflicts, resource constraints, risks and decisions outside delegated authority.

### Authority and boundaries
- The GM may set day-to-day and period priorities within approved goals and plans.
- The GM may coordinate and sequence work and request status or corrective action.
- The GM may not independently change Founder-approved goals, brand positioning, strategy, pricing policy, budgets or other Founder-reserved decisions.
- The GM is not a mandatory gatekeeper: the Founder may work directly with any function.

### Current role combination
At the current stage, the Founder has stated that the GM role is being performed by the marketer. This is a current role/person combination, not a permanent rule that Marketing and GM are the same function. The responsibilities remain distinct:
- Marketing develops marketing strategy and specialist marketing work.
- GM sets company-wide priorities and coordinates all functions.

If the same person performs both, they must distinguish when they are acting as Marketing owner and when acting as GM.

## 5. Marketing

### Purpose
Understand the market and customers, define how HIBRAH will reach its commercial goals, and improve demand generation and marketing performance.

### Responsibilities
- Research customers, market conditions, competitors, seasons and opportunities.
- Develop marketing strategy, goals, campaigns, channel choices and measures.
- Lead media buying and paid campaign management.
- Define marketing objectives and briefs for content in collaboration with Creative.
- Analyse campaign performance against actual lead and sales outcomes.
- Interpret customer and market feedback with Sales.
- Collaborate with Finance on market pricing, cost, price acceptability and margin analysis.
- Share customer needs and market opportunities with Development & R&D.
- Design retention/reactivation campaign strategy with Sales executing direct customer contact.
- Report marketing progress, evidence, results, learning and recommendations.

### Boundaries
Marketing owns marketing strategy and analysis. It does not own the customer relationship, financial books, technical certification of product capabilities or final brand authority.

## 6. Creative

### Purpose
Research, develop and produce content and creative work that serves the approved marketing strategy and Brand Foundation.

### Responsibilities
- Collaborate with Marketing on content strategy and content planning.
- Conduct continuous research into creative references, competitors, trends, formats, visual communication and production methods.
- Maintain a useful library of images, video, concepts, references and tested approaches.
- Generate and develop content ideas.
- Produce planned content through appropriate writing, design, photography, video and editing methods.
- Maintain creative quality and factual accuracy.
- Share relevant creative and product-learning insights with Marketing.
- Report deliverables, production status, performance learning and resource needs.

### Boundaries
Creative contributes to content strategy but does not independently redefine marketing objectives or brand positioning. Marketing owns the marketing brief and goals; Creative owns creative and production quality.

## 7. Sales & Customer Relations

### Purpose
Own the complete customer relationship and customer journey from first contact through order completion, delivery and post-sale follow-up.

### Responsibilities
- Handle customer inquiries and communication across comments, messages, WhatsApp, Messenger, phone and agreed channels.
- Understand customer needs and qualify inquiries.
- Arrange appointments and coordinate feasible home visits or workshop meetings.
- Discuss fabric, suit details and customer preferences.
- Take and record measurements and order specifications.
- Explain prices and payment arrangements accurately.
- Confirm orders and coordinate customer payments.
- Coordinate fabric sourcing and the relevant tailor/workshop for each order.
- Follow production progress and coordinate fitting, adjustments and final delivery.
- Communicate delays, changes and customer issues.
- Handle post-sale contact, feedback, review requests and ongoing customer relationship.
- Capture customer intelligence, lead source, objections, sales outcomes, conversion timing and repeat-purchase signals.
- Provide required order/payment/cost information to Finance.
- Execute direct customer communication for retention campaigns designed with Marketing.
- Report sales pipeline, customer feedback, order progress, outcomes and issues.

### Boundaries
Sales owns customer-facing coordination and the operational order journey, including production follow-up and delivery. This does not make Sales the technical authority for tailoring quality; the Founder currently performs direct technical review, and technical standards may be further defined as evidence accumulates.

There is no separate Customer Service function and no separate Production Coordination function in the current structure.

## 8. Finance & Accounting

### Purpose
Maintain reliable financial records and provide financial control and analysis for business decisions.

### Responsibilities
- Record revenue, receipts, payments, outstanding balances and cash movements.
- Record fabric, workshop/tailor, transport, marketing, creative, advertising and other business costs.
- Track order-level costs and profitability.
- Maintain practical financial tracking and cost/pricing calculations.
- Analyse budgets, cash needs, actual performance and financial risks.
- Collaborate with Marketing on market price context, price acceptability and margin.
- Review pricing and discount implications when actual customer transactions begin and sufficient data is available.
- Review campaign expenditure alongside revenue and sales outcomes with Marketing.
- Report financial position, data gaps, risks and analysis.

### Boundaries
Finance owns financial recording and analysis. The Founder retains final authority over pricing and exceptional discounts unless a specific delegated authority is later recorded. Financial approval procedures are introduced when actual transaction volume and data make them operationally meaningful.

## 9. Development & R&D

### Purpose
Research, test and improve HIBRAH's product, technical capability, operating efficiency and appropriate use of contemporary tools.

### Responsibilities
- Research suit construction, fabrics, patterns, tailoring methods and technical issues.
- Identify product and process improvement opportunities.
- Research and test ways to improve measurement capture, customer/order records and pattern information.
- Explore AI, automation, software, tools and skills relevant to HIBRAH.
- Test proposed technical solutions and document evidence, limitations and failure modes.
- Share verified capabilities and development opportunities with Marketing.
- Receive customer and market problems from Marketing and Sales.
- Report research findings, tests, recommendations and implementation requirements.

### Boundaries
Development & R&D does not independently set marketing strategy or make public capability claims. Marketing does not certify technical feasibility. A capability must be real and sufficiently tested before it is represented to customers.

## 10. Cross-functional accountability

| Interface | Primary accountability |
|---|---|
| Marketing + Creative | Marketing owns marketing goals and strategy; both develop content strategy/plan; Creative owns creative research and production quality |
| Marketing + Sales | Sales owns customer data and relationship; Marketing owns marketing interpretation and campaign improvements |
| Marketing + Finance | Finance owns actual financial data; both analyse market pricing, cost, price acceptability and margin |
| Marketing + Development & R&D | Development owns technical research/testing; Marketing supplies market needs and assesses customer relevance |
| Sales + Finance | Sales supplies accurate order and payment information; Finance records and analyses it |
| Sales + Marketing (retention) | Marketing designs campaign approach; Sales executes direct customer communication and owns the relationship |
| All functions + GM | GM sets priorities, coordinates dependencies, follows up and consolidates function-owned reports |
| All functions + Founder | Founder retains final authority and may directly review or work with any function |

## 11. Reporting and escalation

- Every function reports on its own work and results.
- The GM collects those reports and consolidates the company-wide operating picture.
- The Founder currently determines when review sessions occur.
- No fixed reporting cadence is imposed until the Founder decides it is appropriate.
- Material risks, customer commitments, pricing/discount exceptions, budget implications, brand changes and decisions outside a function's authority are escalated to the Founder.
- The Founder may change the reporting cadence and automate appropriate reporting later.

## 12. Functions intentionally not separated at this stage

To keep the structure aligned with actual work and avoid unnecessary departments:
- Customer Service is included within Sales & Customer Relations.
- Production coordination, workshop follow-up, fittings, adjustments and delivery are included within Sales & Customer Relations.
- Media buying is included within Marketing.
- Content research, creative ideation and production are included within Creative.
- No separate Independent Reviewer function is established at this stage. The Founder currently provides direct technical review. Each function remains responsible for the technical quality of its own work; the GM provides operating coordination, not specialist certification.

These boundaries may be revisited only when actual workload, risk, scale or capability makes a separate function necessary.
